Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Hermes Agent ("the app") is a personal assistant used only by its developer, Lokesh Soni. This policy explains what Google account data the app accesses and what happens to it.

Whose data

The app accesses only the Google account of its developer. It has no other users and collects no data about anyone else through sign-in.

What the app can access

After the developer signs in and gives consent, the app can use these Google permissions on his own account: Gmail (read, send, modify), Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Contacts (read only). It acts only when the developer asks it to.

How the data is used

Data is used only to carry out the developer's own requests, for example summarizing recent email or adding a calendar event. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and it is not sold or shared with third parties.

Where data is processed and stored

  • The app runs on a private server controlled by the developer. Google sign-in tokens are stored on that server with restricted file permissions.
  • To answer a request, relevant content (for example the text of an email being summarized) is sent to the AI model service the developer has chosen, Anthropic's Claude, for processing. It is sent only for that request.
  • Actions the app takes on the Google account are recorded in the developer's private notes.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The developer can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which makes the stored tokens useless. He can also delete the token files from the server.
