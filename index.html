Hermes Agent

A personal AI assistant built and used by one person, Lokesh Soni.

Privacy PolicyTerms of Service

What it is

Hermes Agent is a private, single-user assistant that runs on a server owned by its developer. It uses the Google APIs only to help the developer manage his own Google account: reading and sending his own email, managing his own calendar, and working with his own Drive files, Docs, Sheets and contacts.

Who can use it

Only the developer. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not ask anyone else to sign in. The Google sign-in screen for this app is shown only to the developer's own account.
